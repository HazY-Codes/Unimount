BEHAVIOUR

Manual whitelist mode
Only mount module IDs listed in /data/adb/unimount/modules.txt. Off mounts all eligible overlay modules.
Mirror SELinux contexts
Clones target labels and POSIX permissions before binding. Fails closed when SELinux is Enforcing.
Directory mirror injection
Stages minimal-ancestor mirrors in /data/adb/unimount/mirror to inject new files and folders without OverlayFS or tmpfs.
Skip Zygisk-only modules
Skips overlay scanning for modules without partition folders (system, vendor, product, system_ext, odm, prism, optics).
Bootloop circuit breaker
Skips all mounts after 2 consecutive incomplete boots. Resets once Android finishes booting or after 25s post-mount health check.
Protect critical system paths
Blocks mounts over init, linker*, vold, *servicemanager, and SELinux policy directories.
Verbose log
One line per file bind, mirror stage, or skip in /data/adb/unimount/mount.log. Enabled by default; switch off for quieter logs.

COVERAGE

All changes are applied with read-only bind mounts and minimal-ancestor mirrors under /data/adb/unimount/mirror. Physical partitions are never remounted read-write, leaving Knox 0x0 untouched across soft reboots.
